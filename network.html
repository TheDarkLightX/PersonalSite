Blockchain & crypto

Formal specification Tau / Tau Net

A community built around a decidable, declarative, executable formal specification language. Tau lets you specify software as logical constraints and synthesize correct-by-construction programs. I've contributed experiments, tutorials, and tooling to the ecosystem.

 Layer-1 blockchain PulseChain

An Ethereum fork with lower fees and higher throughput. Active community member since launch, contributing to ecosystem awareness and technical discussion.

 Smart contracts Ethereum

The original smart contract platform. Long-time community participant across protocol design, DeFi, and formal verification of contract systems.

 The original Bitcoin / Bitcointalk

The first crypto community I was part of. Active on Bitcointalk during the formative years of Bitcoin, following protocol development, mining, and the early ecosystem of layered protocols built on top of Bitcoin.

 Bitcoin layers Mastercoin / Omni

One of the earliest token platforms built on Bitcoin. Early contributor during the formative period of layered Bitcoin protocols.

 DEX & DAOs BitShares / Steem

Graphene-based decentralized exchange and social platform. Contributed during the early era of DAO governance and delegated proof-of-stake systems.
