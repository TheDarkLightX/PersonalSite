    What runs here, and what was checked?

    The Rust app was emitted by the pinned ZenoFCIS 2.1 development factory. Its native tests exercise the actual Authority and SQLite shell. This page uses 384 recorded Authority answers; its JavaScript and the export adapter are tested, not formally verified.

    The finite model has 14 reachable states and 112 outgoing state/action/caller combinations. A separate Lean model proves preservation and at most one release intent for every finite history. The Lean model’s class, successor state, and release count also match all 384 Authority rows in an executable check; no Rust-to-Lean compiler is proved.

    The selector supplies an ID. A deployed service would have to authenticate that ID, protect the database, and implement a durable idempotent destination. A hostile host can alter delivery acknowledgements or restore an old valid database. No real payment or network delivery happens here.
